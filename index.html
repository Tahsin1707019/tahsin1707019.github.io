TÜBİTAK Project 125E059

Machine unlearning as a backdoor defense

I benchmarked five unlearning methods on BERT classifiers across three datasets and four settings and proposed HRMU, a hybrid unlearning method. In the main experiments, all five methods reduced attack success rates to near zero and kept accuracy close to clean-model baselines. HRMU performed best in most cases.

Defending LLM classifiers

I am evaluating the same unlearning methods as defenses for backdoored large language models on classification tasks. I measure how well they suppress backdoor behavior and preserve accuracy on clean inputs.

What remains after unlearning

I am investigating whether models retain enough information to reconstruct backdoor triggers after unlearning. This work examines whether reduced attack success is accompanied by removal of the underlying information.
